About NSS Security Agency
A Trusted Leader in Security, Vigilance & Guarding

NSS Security Agency Jharkhand is a professional security agency located at Bypass Road, Mahto Market, Chas, Bokaro, Jharkhand. Under the leadership of our Director / Managing Director, Mr. Dilip Kumar, we supply trained and disciplined security and manpower for a wide range of clients.

Our motto — Safety Today… Tomorrow — guides how we recruit, train and deploy our people.

Who we serve

  • Corporate offices and commercial establishments
  • Industrial plants and factories
  • Banks, ATMs and healthcare facilities
  • Educational institutions and residential societies

Mission & Vision

Our Mission — To provide dependable security and manpower services that protect people, property and business continuity for every client.

Our Vision — To be the most trusted security agency in Jharkhand, known for discipline, reliability and a service-first attitude.

Discipline · Loyalty · Security · Service — these four words define every NSS deployment.
