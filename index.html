A Trusted Leader in Security, Vigilance & Guarding Services

NSS provides Security Guards, Gunmen, PSO, Patrolling and Manpower Solutions, which are essential to the safety and smooth functioning of industries, institutions and homes. We have emerged as a trusted name in security services in Bokaro and across Jharkhand.

With a disciplined workforce and a service-first approach, NSS Security Agency combines trained people, clear procedures and regular supervision to deliver dependable protection to every client site.

Our foundation is rooted in manpower delivery — verified, uniformed and briefed guards.
